The goal for this tutorial is to showcase step-by-step how to upload images to the DaTe website. 
The tutorial is done on a Windows machine.

The tutorial will show you how to upload images to three different locations:
1. to the "bildarkiv", for images from events
2. to the front page, for the logos of our partners ("samarbetspartners")
3. to the "halarsponsorer", for the quick presentations of our overall sponsors

1. Bildarkiv. 

Navigate to admin page on the website. Here choose "bildarkiv" under "Archive".
